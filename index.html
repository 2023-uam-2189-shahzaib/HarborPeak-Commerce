Three-Stage Framework

Our Partnership Model

Clear processes, defined responsibilities, and transparent commercial agreements for e-commerce partnerships.

01 Stage 1

Consultation & Eligibility Review

  • Understand the partner's business goals.
  • Explain the partnership model and services.
  • Review eligibility and operational requirements.
  • Answer questions before any commitment.
02 Stage 2

Agreement & Store Preparation

  • Provide a written partnership agreement.
  • Clearly define ownership, responsibilities, expenses, and commission terms.
  • Explain platform requirements and account access.
  • Allow partners to review the agreement before signing.
03 Stage 3

Operations & Reporting

  • Coordinate store operations and marketing.
  • Provide regular performance updates.
  • Maintain transparent records of sales, expenses, refunds, and commissions.
  • Provide monthly financial statements according to the agreement.
💰

Clear Commission Terms

Every commercial arrangement is explicitly contracted in writing prior to store launch.

The written partnership agreement must specify:

  • Commission percentage and calculation method.
  • Eligible sales and deductions.
  • Advertising and operational expenses.
  • Refunds, chargebacks, and payment timing.
  • Responsibilities of each party.
